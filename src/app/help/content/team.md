# Team

**Team** lists everyone in the workspace in two groups: **Team members**, and **Managers & Admins**.

## Add people

People join by **invite**. Managers can invite Team members; Admins can invite anyone, as a Team member, Manager or Admin.

1. Press **+ Add person** and choose **Team member**, **Manager** or **Admin**.
2. Enter their email and press **Create invite link**.
3. pip emails them the link and also shows it to you **once**. If the email couldn't be sent, pip says so; copy the link and send it yourself.
4. They open the link, set their name and password, and they're in. See [Your first login](/help/first-login).

Each link works once and expires (after 7 days by default). Until someone accepts, their invite is listed under **Pending invites** with its expiry date. Use **Cancel invite** to withdraw it. You can't invite an email that's already in the workspace.

**No email?** In the invite dialog, choose **Set a temporary password instead**. You create the account with a password you share with them yourself.

Once a Team member has joined, give them [access](#access-and-visibility) to the projects they'll work on. Adding Team members can change your [plan](/help/trial-and-plans).

## Access and visibility

Team members only see what you give them. There are three controls:

- **Access → Projects:** the projects they're assigned to. This is the same as the project's **Team** on the Projects tab.
- **Access → Client visibility:** clients where they can see *all* projects and team activity without being assigned to each one.
- **Visibility: Their access / All clients:** the button in the table. **All clients** lets them see every client, whatever their access.

Active [holiday cover](/help/holiday-cover) also gives temporary access.

## Edit, deactivate, reactivate

From the **⋯** menu:

- **Edit:** change their name. Admins can also change the role.
- **Deactivate:** they can no longer sign in. Their time stays in reports. Tick **Show deactivated** to see them, and use **Reactivate** to restore them.

Managers can only manage Team members. Nobody can change their own role or deactivate themselves.

## Member pages

Click a name to open their page, with hours, billable share, hours by client, recent entries, access and their change **History** for this week or this month.

## Requests for access

When a team member presses **Ask to be added to projects**, a **Waiting for access** box appears at the top of the Team page, and Team in the sidebar shows a count.

- **Give access** opens their access settings. Once you save them with at least one project or client, the request closes.
- **Dismiss** closes the request without giving them access.
