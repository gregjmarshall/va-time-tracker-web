# Projects

Managers and Admins see every project on the **Projects** tab, with its budget, team and recent activity.

## The table

- **This month:** everyone's hours this month.
- **Total vs budget:** all-time hours against the budget. The bar is teal, turns yellow at 80% and coral when over.
- **Team:** who's assigned. Click the avatars to change it.
- **Last tracked:** when anyone last tracked time to it.

Search by project or client, filter by client, and switch between **Active** and **Archived**.

## Create or edit

- **+ New project:** pick a client, then add a name and an optional **budget** in hours. Leave the budget empty for an open-ended project.
- **⋯ → Edit:** change the name or budget.

## Team

**⋯ → Manage team** lists Team members. Tick the people who work on the project and save. Assigned people can see and track time to it. This is the same list as the *Projects* tab in a person's [Access](/help/team#access-and-visibility).

## Archive

**⋯ → Archive** hides a project. Archived projects keep their time, but nobody can track time to them. Restore a project from **Archived**.

## History

**⋯ → History** is the project's audit trail: who created it, renamed it, changed the budget or team, or archived or restored it, and when. See [Audit trail](/help/audit-trail).
