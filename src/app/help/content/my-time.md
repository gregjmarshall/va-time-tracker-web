# My Time

**My Time** is your personal report: every hour you've logged, for any period.

## Choose a period

Pick a preset (**Year to date**, **Month to date**, **Last month**, **This week**, **Last week**, **Today**, **Yesterday**) or **Custom…** for your own dates.

## Filters

- **Client** and **project**.
- **Billable & non-billable**, **Billable only** or **Non-billable only**.
- **Holiday cover:** including, excluding or only cover time.

## Tabs

- **Summary:** totals, a billable vs non-billable chart and hours per project.
- **Detailed:** every entry, one row each.

## Export

- **CSV:** every entry in the current filters, for spreadsheets.
- **PDF:** the chart, summary and every entry. It opens a print window, so choose *Save as PDF*.

All hours are [rounded](/help/rounding-and-billable) hours.
