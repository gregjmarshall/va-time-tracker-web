# Reports and exports

**Reports** shows hours for any period and anyone. Team members have the same page as [My Time](/help/my-time), limited to their own hours.

## Period and filters

- **Period:** presets from *Today* to *Year to date*, or **Custom…** for your own dates.
- **Client**, **project** and **person** (*Everyone* by default).
- **Billable:** both, billable only, or non-billable only.
- **Holiday cover:** including, excluding, or cover only.

The line under the title sums up the current filters, for example *Last month · Acme · billable only*.

## Tabs

- **Summary:** total, billable and non-billable hours, a chart by day, week or month, and hours per client and project.
- **Detailed:** every entry with date, time, person, project and billable status, 50 per page.
- **Retainers:** this month's hours against each retainer.

## Export

- **CSV:** every entry in the current filters, one row each.
- **PDF:** the chart, summary and entries in a print window. Choose *Save as PDF*.

All figures use [rounded](/help/rounding-and-billable) hours.
