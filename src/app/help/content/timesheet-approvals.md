# Timesheet approvals

Managers and Admins see a **Team** tab on the **Timesheet** page.

## Review a week

1. Open **Timesheet → Team** and pick the week.
2. The table shows each person's **status**, **hours**, **billable** hours and when they submitted. The line above it says how many are waiting.
3. Press **Review** on a submitted week, or **Open** on any other.

You can also choose a person from the drop-down next to the tabs.

## Decide

- **Approve:** the week stays locked.
- **Reject:** enter a reason (*What needs fixing?*). The person sees it, can edit the week and resubmit.
- **Withdraw:** Admins can pull a submitted week back to Draft for the person.
- **Reopen:** Admins only. Moves an approved week back to Draft.

Managers can't approve their own week; an Admin can. Admins can also edit time in a locked week directly.

## Who appears

Every active person in the workspace. People who haven't submitted show as **Draft**.
