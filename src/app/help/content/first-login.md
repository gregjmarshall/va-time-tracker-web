# Your first login

## Joining your agency

Team members can't sign themselves up. An Admin or Manager invites you from the **Team** page.

1. Open the invite link from the email pip sent you, or from your manager.
2. Enter **your name** and **choose a password**. Your email is already filled in.
3. Press **Accept invite**. You're signed in and land on **Overview**, where a **Welcome** card walks you through the basics.
4. Check **Projects** to see what you can track time to. If you're not on any yet, press **Ask to be added to projects**. Your manager sees the request on their **Team** page. When they add you, a note appears on your Overview and your projects show up in the timer straight away.

An invite link works **once** and expires (after 7 days by default). If it says the invite has expired, was cancelled or has already been used, ask whoever invited you for a new link.

Some agencies set you up with a **temporary password** instead. In that case, sign in with your email and that password.

## Signing in

Sign in with your email and password. If your email belongs to more than one agency, pip asks *which one?* Pick the agency you want to work in.

## Starting a new agency

To try pip for your own agency, choose **Start a free trial** on the sign-in page. See [Trial and plans](/help/trial-and-plans#start-an-agency).

## Changing your password

There's no change-password screen in the app yet. To set your own password:

1. Sign out (the arrow next to your name, bottom left).
2. On the sign-in page, choose **Forgot password?**
3. Enter your email. pip sends you a reset link, so check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive.

## Finding your way around

- **On a phone:** open the menu with the ☰ button at the top left.
- The **timer bar** is always at the top, so you can start and stop time from any page.
- If your timer is running, the pip logo in the sidebar has a moving teal ring.
