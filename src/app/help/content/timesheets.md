# Timesheets

The **Timesheet** shows one week as a grid, with projects down the side and Monday to Sunday across the top. Each cell is the time you logged to that project that day, including timer and manual entries.

## Fill in your week

1. Use ‹ and › to change week, or **This week** to jump back.
2. Click a cell and type hours as **1.5**, **1:30** or **90m**. Press Enter or click away to save.
3. To add a row, choose a project from **Choose a project…** under the grid.
4. Add a note to a cell with its note button. If your workspace requires descriptions, pip asks for a note when you add new time.

Typing a number sets the cell's total. You can't type less than the time already tracked by the timer or manual entries; edit those entries instead.

## Submit

Press **Submit week** when the week is done. Its status becomes **Submitted** and the week **locks**: nobody except an Admin can add or change time in it, from the grid, the timer or a manual entry.

- **Withdraw:** pulls a submitted week back to Draft so you can edit it.
- **Rejected:** your manager has sent it back. Their reason is shown above the grid. Fix it and press **Resubmit week**.
- **Approved:** the week stays locked. Only an Admin can reopen it.

| Status | Can you edit? |
|---|---|
| Draft | Yes |
| Submitted | No, unless you withdraw it |
| Rejected | Yes |
| Approved | No |

For Managers: see [Timesheet approvals](/help/timesheet-approvals).
