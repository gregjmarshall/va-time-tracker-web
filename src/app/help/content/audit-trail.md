# Audit trail

pip records who changed what, and when, for projects and people.

## Where to find it

- **Projects:** **⋯ → History** on a project. It records creation, renames, budget changes, team changes, and archiving or restoring.
- **People:** the **History** section on a team member's page. It records role, access and status changes, such as deactivation.
- **Retainers:** the **History** list in the retainer dialog shows every allowance change by month.

Each event shows who did it, a short summary and the time.

> **Note:** History starts from when the feature was added. Older changes don't appear. There isn't one workspace-wide audit log yet.
