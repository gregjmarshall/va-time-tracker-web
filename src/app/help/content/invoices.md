# Invoices

Invoices are sent **manually** for now. There's no billing page in the app yet.

- Invoices for your plan are issued by pip outside the app.
- To change plan, switch to annual billing or get a copy of an invoice, contact pip.

> **Note:** This page is about paying for pip. To bill **your own clients**, use [Reports](/help/reports) and export a CSV or PDF for the client and month.
