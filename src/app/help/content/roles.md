# Roles

Every person in a workspace has one of three roles. The role controls what they can see and change.

## Team member

- Tracks time with the timer, manual entries and the timesheet.
- Sees only the clients and projects they have access to (see [Team](/help/team#access-and-visibility)).
- Sees their own hours in **My Time** and **My projects**.
- Submits their weekly timesheet.
- Can't change whether an entry is billable.

## Manager

Everything a Team member can do, plus:

- Clients, projects, budgets and retainers.
- Adding Team members, and changing their access, names and status.
- Reports for everyone, timesheet approvals, Capacity and Holiday cover.
- Choosing whether an entry is billable.

Managers **can't** add Managers or Admins, change roles, or see client rates. They can't approve their own timesheet.

## Admin

Everything a Manager can do, plus:

- Adding Managers and Admins, and changing anyone's role.
- Client **rates and payment methods**, which only Admins can see.
- Workspace **Settings**, such as requiring a description on every entry.
- Reopening approved timesheets, and editing time in locked weeks.

> **Note:** Nobody can change their own role or deactivate themselves, and a workspace always keeps at least one active Admin.
