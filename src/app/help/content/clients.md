# Clients

**Clients** lists every client with this month's hours, retainer status and projects. It's for Managers and Admins. Team members see a read-only list of their own clients.

## Add a client

Press **+ New client**, then enter a name and an optional contact email.

## Client card

- **Retainer**, **Ad hoc** or **Over:** the retainer state this month, with a usage bar.
- **Last tracked**, tenure (from **Client since**) and, for Admins only, the rate and payment method.
- **Overseen by:** the Managers responsible for the client. Click it to change. Managers see **My clients** by default.
- **Edit details:** name, contact email and *Client since*. Admins also set the **rate** (hourly or monthly, in £) and the **payment method** (direct debit, advance retainer or arrears).
- **Set retainer / Edit retainer:** see [Retainers](/help/retainers).
- **Projects:** expand to see each project's budget. **+ Add project** adds one (ongoing, or fixed with budget hours).

## Find a client

Search by name, filter by **Retainer**, **Ad hoc** or **Tracked in last 30 days**, and sort by name or most recently tracked.
