# FAQ and troubleshooting

## I can't see a client or project

You only see what you're assigned to. Ask a Manager to check your [access](/help/team#access-and-visibility). If you were covering a client, the cover may have ended.

## Start is greyed out on My projects

A timer is already running or paused. Stop or discard it in the timer bar first.

## "This week's timesheet is submitted or approved, so its time is locked."

You've submitted that week. **Withdraw** it on the Timesheet to make changes, or ask an Admin if it's approved. See [Timesheets](/help/timesheets).

## Stop won't save my time

Your workspace requires a description. Type one in the timer bar, then press Stop.

## I can't tick Billable

Only Managers and Admins can change billable status.

## Why is the retainer burn "Too early"?

Fewer than two working days of the month have passed. Weekends and UK bank holidays don't count.

## The hours in Reports are different from the timer

Reports use **rounded** hours, and filters such as billable or holiday cover may be on. Check the line under the title.

## I forgot my password

Choose **Forgot password?** on the sign-in page. If the email doesn't arrive, check your spam folder.

## "Account is deactivated."

An Admin or Manager has deactivated your account. Ask them to reactivate it.

## Someone left the agency

Deactivate them on **Team**. Their time stays in reports, and they no longer count towards your [plan](/help/trial-and-plans).

## My invite link doesn't work

Invite links work once and expire (after 7 days by default). They can also be cancelled. Ask whoever invited you for a new link.

## pip asks which agency to sign in to

Your email is in more than one agency. Pick the one you want; to switch, sign out and sign in again.
