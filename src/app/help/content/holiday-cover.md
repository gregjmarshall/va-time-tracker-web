# Holiday cover

Holiday cover is when someone looks after another person's clients for a while, usually while they're on holiday.

## If you're covering

- Your **Overview** shows a **Holiday cover** card with the client, the dates and the hours so far.
- While the cover is **active**, you can see that client and track time to its projects as usual.
- That time is labelled **Holiday cover** in your entries and reports.
- After the cover ends, the client disappears again unless you have your own access to it.

## For Managers and Admins

Open **Holiday cover** in the sidebar.

1. Press **+ Add cover**.
2. Choose **who's covering** and one or more **clients**.
3. Set the **start** date and when it **ends**: end of week, end of month, a date, or no end date.
4. Add an optional note, for example *Covering while Sarah is on holiday*.

Covers show as **Upcoming**, **Active** or **Ended**. From the list you can change the end date, **End today**, **Cancel cover** (if it hasn't started) or delete it.

- Ending a cover early keeps the time already logged as cover.
- **Deleting** a cover means that time is no longer reported as cover.

## How cover time is counted

- It **still counts** towards the client's totals and retainer.
- **Reports** can include, exclude or show only cover time, so it can be billed separately.
- In **Capacity**, cover is shown separately and doesn't use up the person's own allocation.
