# What is pip?

pip is a time tracker for virtual assistant (VA) agencies. Your team tracks time against clients and projects. Managers then see where the hours went, how each retainer is being used and who has room for more work.

## The main ideas

- **Clients** are the businesses your agency works for.
- **Projects** belong to a client. All time is tracked to a project.
- **Retainers** are a monthly allowance of hours, either for a whole client or for a single project.
- **Time entries** come from the timer, a manual entry or the weekly timesheet. All three end up in the same place.
- **Holiday cover** lets someone look after another person's clients for a while, and the time is labelled as cover.

## Where things are

- **Overview:** your day at a glance. For Managers and Admins, it shows the agency's hours and retainer burn.
- **Timer bar:** at the top of every page.
- **Timesheet:** a weekly grid of your hours, for submitting and approving.
- **My Time / Reports:** hours for any date range, with CSV and PDF exports.
- **Projects** and **Clients:** what you can track time to.
- **Team**, **Capacity** and **Holiday cover:** for Managers and Admins.

> **Tip:** Look for the **?** icon next to a page title. It opens the help for that screen.
