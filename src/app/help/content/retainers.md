# Retainers

A retainer is a monthly allowance of hours. Clients without one are **ad hoc**.

## Client-wide or per project

Open **Set retainer** (or **Edit retainer**) on a client and choose what it **applies to**:

- **Whole client:** covers every hour on the client *except* projects with their own retainer.
- **Project:** its own allowance. Hours on that project count here, not towards the client-wide retainer.

A client can have both: a client-wide retainer and some projects with their own.

## Set, change or end a retainer

1. Choose what it applies to.
2. Enter the **monthly hours** and the month it starts **from**.
3. Press **Set retainer** or **Change retainer**.

To stop it, press **Switch to ad hoc**, which ends it from the chosen month.

Changes always take effect from the **1st of a month**. Earlier months keep their old allowance, so past reports don't change.

## History

- **Used vs allowance:** a bar for each of the last 12 months, showing hours used against the allowance. Teal is within the allowance, coral is over, and the line marks the allowance. The current month is marked *so far*.
- **History:** every change, for example *40h → 60h from Mar 2026*, and when it went ad hoc.

## Retainer burn

Managers and Admins see **Retainer burn** on the Overview. It shows how each retainer is tracking this month against an even pace over the month's **working days**. Weekends and **UK bank holidays** (England and Wales) don't count as working days.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| On pace | At least 90% of where an even pace would be by yesterday |
| Behind | 70–90% of even pace |
| At risk | Below 70%: likely to go unused |
| Over | More hours than the allowance |
| Too early | Fewer than 2 working days have passed this month |

Each row also shows **Needs Xh/day for N working days**: the hours per working day, from today, needed to use the rest of the allowance.

> **Note:** The **Retainers** tab in Reports uses simpler labels: *On track*, *Near limit* (over 80% used) and *Over*.
